Wash day

How to use the range together. Most hair does well with a wash day once a week or every other week.

  1. Step 1

    Cleanse

    Work the shampoo into your scalp with your fingertips, then rinse well. Keep the lengths gentle, as they need less washing than the scalp.

  2. Step 2

    Condition

    Smooth the conditioner through damp hair from mid-length to ends. Leave it on for 15–30 minutes under a cap, then rinse with cool water.

  3. Step 3

    Moisturise

    While your hair is still damp, work a little moisturiser through the lengths, focusing on the ends where hair is driest.

  4. Step 4

    Seal & feed

    Part your hair into sections and massage the oil into your scalp. Run what's left over the ends to seal in moisture. Repeat two or three times a week.

About us

Nonhle's Cosmetics makes hair care from natural ingredients: shea butter, black African soap, pure oils, essential oils and herbs. There are no parabens or sulfates in anything we make.

The range covers a full wash day, from shampoo and deep conditioner to moisturiser and our Hairgrowth Oil. It works on every hair type, from tight coils to loose waves, and on relaxed hair and protective styles.

We also do braids, makeup and nails.
